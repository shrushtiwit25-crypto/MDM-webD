Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for admission? ▼
You can apply for admission through the official college admission portal by filling out the application form.
What documents are required for admission? ▼
Students need their academic marksheets, identity proof, photographs and other documents mentioned by the college.
How can I check my examination schedule? ▼
The examination schedule can be checked through the college website or student portal.
Where can I find study materials? ▼
Study materials are available on the student portal and can also be obtained from the college library.
How can I contact my department? ▼
You can contact your department through the official college email, phone number or department office.
